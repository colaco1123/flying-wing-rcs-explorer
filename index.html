What this model is and isn't

Everything is treated as perfect conductor. In parametric mode the wing is a zero-thickness plate. For each look angle the physical-optics surface integral is evaluated in closed form as a sum over polygon edges (Gordon's method), so spike widths of about λ/(2L) are resolved without meshing. Radar sits at the chosen elevation above the plate plane; RCS scales with sin² of that angle in this model.

  • Good for: comparing planform layouts at the same frequency and elevation, and checking where edge-normal spikes fall. Relative changes are meaningful.
  • Misses: edge diffraction and travelling-wave returns, which dominate near grazing where PO goes to zero, so absolute dBsm at low elevation is optimistic. No thickness, inlets, exhausts, cavities, gaps, antennas or RAM. Those usually set the real number.
  • STEP mode: the CAD model is tessellated in your browser and every lit facet is integrated exactly and summed coherently. Back faces are culled, but there is no shadowing between parts and no multiple bounces, so inlets, cavities and dihedral corners are underestimated. Curved surfaces become flat facets, which adds small false glints; use Fine tessellation to check a result holds.
  • Hot-spot colours: each facet is coloured by its backscattered power per unit area, relative to the hottest facets. That shows specular flashes from flat and curved surfaces well. It does not show edge diffraction or corner (multiple-bounce) returns, because physical optics has neither.
  • Symmetry: a flat plate in PO returns the same from φ and φ+180°, so nose and tail sectors always match in parametric mode. STEP mode breaks this with real thickness and camber. Real nose/tail differences come from thickness, inlets and exhausts.
  • Validity: needs the shape to be many wavelengths across. Below about 5λ span (low L-band on a small UAV) treat the output as qualitative only.

Flying Wing RCS Explorer

Physical optics after W. B. Gordon (1975) for polygon and facet integrals, checked against the POFACETS formulation (D. C. Jenn, Naval Postgraduate School). STEP files are read with occt-import-js (OpenCascade) and drawn with three.js. Everything runs in your browser; no geometry is uploaded.
